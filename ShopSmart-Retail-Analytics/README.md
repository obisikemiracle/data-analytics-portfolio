## Key Areas of Analysis

- Product and category revenue
- Store and regional sales performance
- Monthly sales trends
- Customer spending
- Product returns
- Inventory levels
- Business risks and growth opportunities

## Key Findings

The Beauty category generated the highest revenue among product categories analysed. ShopSmart Lagos recorded the highest store sales, while the East region recorded the highest regional sales. The analysis also identified inventory monitoring and data quality as areas requiring management attention.

## Business Recommendations

Recommendations include investing strategically in high-performing product categories, learning from successful stores and regions, improving inventory management, and strengthening supplier and product-return records.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to analyse retail data, visualise business performance, identify issues, and develop data-driven recommendations for ShopSmart Retail Ltd.
